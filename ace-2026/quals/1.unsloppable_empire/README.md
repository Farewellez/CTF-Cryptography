## "Unsloppable" Empire
### author: majukemajuan49

The Empire proudly claims that its brand-new encryption system is impossible to break.

As a member of the Rebel Alliance, your mission is to infiltrate the Emperor's Vault and recover the secret hidden within.

Imperial engineers insist their system is secure, but rumors from intercepted transmissions suggest that identical messages produce... identical outcomes.

Can you forge a ticket worthy of the Emperor? May the Force be with you.
