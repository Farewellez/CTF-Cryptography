## "Unsloppable" Jedi Archive
### author: majukemajuan49

The Empire has intercepted a sealed Jedi archive belonging to a famous survivor.

The record is protected with RSA, but the modulus is hidden inside the Imperial system. Fortunately, an internal audit interface was left exposed. It does not reveal the modulus directly, but it may answer carefully crafted comparisons.

Observe the service, collect what the Empire leaks, and reconstruct the lost Jedi message.

The archive cannot be solved by a single prophecy.
